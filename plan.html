The plan

The business, in one page.

A public summary of my business plan. Prices are rough starting points that I'll test with my first clients.

😩 The problem

  • Lots of cafés, bakeries, salons, gyms and tradies on the Beaches have an old website, or none
  • Their Instagram is quiet because owners are busy
  • Their online shop window looks tired

💡 The offer

  • A modern website in about 2 weeks
  • A 'living website' that updates from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook events and Google reviews
  • Fresh Instagram posts every month

🎯 Who it's for

  • Local businesses on Sydney's Northern Beaches
  • Brookvale trades and workshops, Freshwater and Manly cafés and bakeries
  • Salons, gyms and tradies who want to look great online

Packages

Website build · A$1,500–3,000 one-off

  • Example: A$1,800
  • Design + build, mobile-friendly
  • Google Maps, contact or order form
  • 2 rounds of changes, about 2 weeks

Website care · A$99/month

  • Hosting, updates
  • Menu and price changes, small fixes
  • A monthly check that everything works

Instagram care · A$300–500/month

  • 12 posts + stories
  • Captions, hashtags, scheduling
  • One-page monthly report

How I find clients

🚶 Walk in

  • At a quiet time, not the lunch rush
  • Show the concept on my phone
  • Offer a free website + Instagram check and leave a card

💬 Follow up

  • A friendly Instagram DM or email a few days later
  • Keep it short, stop if they say no
  • Ask if I can check back in a couple of months

🤝 Look after clients

  • A 15-minute check-in every month
  • Go through the report, plan next month's posts
  • Happy clients → referrals
